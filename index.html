ℹ️ About FitForm AI

An AI fitness form checker that uses your camera, detects body joints in real time and gives live voice correction. Everything runs on your phone – your video is never uploaded.

How it works

  1. Camera Input – captures your exercise movement.
  2. Pose Estimation – MediaPipe detects 33 body keypoints.
  3. Form Analysis – joint angles are checked against rules.
  4. Rep Counting – counts reps and tracks time.
  5. Form Degradation – compares first reps with last reps to detect fatigue.
  6. Voice Assistant – real-time spoken corrections.
  7. Website Output – history, progress charts and summary.

📷 Camera tips

  • Keep the phone steady, side view, 1.5–2 m away.
  • Head to feet should be visible with good light.
  • Move slowly and with control: one clean rep takes about 2 seconds.

🛠 Tech used

MediaPipe Pose Landmarker · JavaScript · HTML/CSS · Web Speech API · GitHub Pages
